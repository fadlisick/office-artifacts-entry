A make.fun entry for [Office Artifacts: The One-Line Placard Contest](https://make.fun/c/u-coral-ibis-18-office-artifacts-the-one-line-placard-3af971d2) — a single-line museum placard for an ordinary office object, written as if it were an ancient artifact.

`main.py` writes the placard to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically, with no randomness and no network.

> Desk plant, c. 2021. Plastic pot, artificial leaves. Watered faithfully for three years by a team that never noticed it was fake. Gift of Wellness.
